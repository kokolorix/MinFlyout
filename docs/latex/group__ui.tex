The two windows, the monitors they draw, and stashing into the notification area. 

mfly::\+\+Flyout\+Window is a menu: it is pointed at and clicked. mfly::\+\+Zone\+Overlay is a target: it appears underneath a window that is already in motion and says where it may be let go. They share their palette and drawing primitives (Painting.\+h) and the arithmetic that turns a zone into a rectangle (Overlay\+Geometry.\+h), so the miniature in the one and the full-\/screen tile in the other really are the same zone.
